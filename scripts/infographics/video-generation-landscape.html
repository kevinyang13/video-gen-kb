The AI video stack, in four layers

Models are weights. A harness runs them. An app wraps a harness in a GUI. A platform rents closed or open models by the clip. Most confusion (“OpenArt vs Seedance”) is comparing across layers.

Layer 4 · top
Websites / platformsRent clips of closed models (Seedance, Veo, Sora, Kling) or open ones via API. Cheapest way to try a model once.
Layer 3
Local appsGUI around a harness. Draw Things is the Mac default; Topaz for paid upscaling; ffmpeg glues it all.
Layer 2
HarnessesLoad weights and run them. ComfyUI is the standard; Draw Things engine and MLX are Metal-native.
Layer 1 · foundation
ModelsOpen weights (Wan, LTX, Hunyuan) run on a Mac. Closed models (Seedance, Veo, Sora, Kling) are the quality ceiling — API only.
Where this project sitsWan 2.2 · LTX-2.3 → Draw Things engine / MLX → Draw Things + REAL Video Enhancer + ffmpeg. Local, open, Apple Silicon. Closed models are a per-clip fallback via fal.ai for hero shots.
